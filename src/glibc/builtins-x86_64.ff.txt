    gcc's builtins of libgcc's unwinder (unwind-dw2.c), for ttpc's frames on x86_64. A C function's rbp holds its caller's rbp at rbp and its return address at rbp + 8, its CFA is rbp + 16 (ttpc -g's .eh_frame:

    __builtin_eh_return: gcc's epilogue of a function that installs a context (_Unwind_RaiseException, _Unwind_ForcedUnwind, _Unwind_Resume ...): it restores the target frame's registers and jumps to its landing pad. Only a frame with a personality
    routine has one, and ttpc writes none (no LSDA): no unwind of ttpc's frames comes here, and glibc's cancellation longjmps out of its stop function. A gcc frame's landing pad (C++'s, lane A-cxx) takes the exception in rax and rdx and its
    callee-saved registers from the frames between, which ttpc's frame does not keep yet: until then it stops here, as int3.

    int3
