    __builtin_eh_return: gcc's epilogue of a function that installs a context (_Unwind_RaiseException, _Unwind_ForcedUnwind, _Unwind_Resume ...): it restores the target frame's registers and jumps to its landing pad. Only a frame with a personality
    routine has one, and ttpc writes none (no LSDA): no unwind of ttpc's frames comes here, and glibc's cancellation longjmps out of its stop function. A gcc frame's landing pad (C++'s, lane A-cxx) takes the exception in a0 and a1 and its
    callee-saved registers from the frames between, which ttpc's frame does not keep yet: until then it stops here, as __builtin_trap.
  __builtin_eh_return:
    asm
    ebreak
