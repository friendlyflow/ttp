  comment
    rv64: ttpc's own instructions, RV64 in ff order (the destination last), in arm instructions (A32): what ttpc, written in RV64, is assembled with to run on arm. mkhost.pl renames each op of ttpc to rv.op and each register to rv.reg.
    Each RV64 register is a slot of 8 bytes, lo word then hi word, at r10 + 8 * its number: a 64-bit value is two registers of arm. A value is loaded into r0:r1 (and r2:r3), worked on in pairs (adds and adc, ...), stored back.
